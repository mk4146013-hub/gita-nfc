A story in two minutes

On the battlefield, Arjuna looked at the people he loved and felt overwhelmed. Before acting, he admitted his confusion to Krishna. The first step toward clarity was not pretending to be fearless—it was asking for guidance.

Takeaway: You do not need every answer before you begin. Start by being honest about what is troubling you.
